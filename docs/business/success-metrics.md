# Success Metrics

## Purpose

This document defines how platform success should be evaluated without reducing the brand to short-term conversion metrics alone.

## Initial Content

## Primary Metrics

Primary metrics:

* Corporate consultation requests.
* Workshop inquiries.
* Calendly bookings.

These metrics directly indicate lead generation and booking performance.

## Secondary Metrics

Secondary metrics:

* Scroll depth.
* Time on site.
* Return visits.
* Social profile visits.

These metrics indicate engagement, trust building, and brand discovery.

## Analytics Events to Track

Track:

* CTA clicks.
* Form submissions.
* Calendly interactions.
* Service exploration.
* Scroll depth.
* Conversion events.

## Decision Priority

When optimizing the product, prioritize:

1. Lead generation
2. Trust
3. Authority
4. Performance

## Measurement Philosophy

Success should not be measured only by services sold or leads generated. The platform should also change how visitors understand the role of image, identity, confidence, and presence in personal and professional transformation.
