# Business Rules

## Purpose

This document defines rules that should constrain product, design, content, and implementation decisions.

## Initial Content

## Core Rule

Every major decision should reinforce the foundational belief:

> Image is not superficial. It is psychological.

## Positioning Rules

* Position Fefi Prieto primarily around psychology and image consulting.
* Treat executive presence as a natural extension of image psychology.
* Treat leadership development as a longer-term evolution, not the initial center of gravity.
* Avoid positioning the brand as generic fashion, coaching, or leadership consulting.
* Maintain clear distinction between Fefi Prieto and Dress the City.

## Conversion Rules

* Corporate inquiry paths should be clear and credible.
* Individual booking paths should remain easy to find.
* CTAs should match visitor intent and journey stage.
* Do not force all visitors into the same conversion path.

## Scope Rules

Do not implement MVP features that belong to later phases unless explicitly approved and documented.

Out-of-scope MVP capabilities are listed in [../product/roadmap.md](../product/roadmap.md).

## Content Rules

* Do not hardcode business content inside UI components.
* Keep service, methodology, navigation, and hero content in dedicated content modules when implementation begins.
* Use educational and psychologically grounded language.
* Avoid trend-focused fashion language.
* Avoid generic motivational language without a clear connection to image psychology.

## Design Rules

* Design should feel premium, editorial, restrained, and intentional.
* Motion should support hierarchy, understanding, storytelling, or emotional impact.
* Visual ambition must not compromise performance, accessibility, or usability.
