# Roadmap

## Purpose

This document defines the product roadmap at a capability level. It prevents future-phase ideas from leaking into the MVP before they are justified.

## Initial Content

## MVP Purpose

The MVP exists to validate:

* Positioning
* Messaging
* Lead generation
* Brand authority

## MVP Scope

The MVP should support:

* Premium brand storytelling.
* Clear explanation of image psychology.
* Individual booking generation.
* Service exploration.
* Trust-building content.
* Performance-focused responsive experience.
* Spanish and English versions of all pages, with Spanish as the default and English under `/en/` (approved 2026-11-23).

## Explicitly Out of MVP Scope

Do not introduce:

* CMS integrations
* Blog infrastructure
* CRM integrations
* User authentication
* User accounts
* Payment processing
* E-commerce
* Membership systems
* Online courses
* Dashboards
* Client portals
* Complex admin panels
* Webinar platforms
* AI-powered personalization
* Mobile applications

## Phase 2 Candidates

Phase 2 may include:

* Educational articles
* Downloadable resources
* Newsletter
* Expanded testimonials
* CMS adoption if justified

These should be added only after MVP learning shows they support authority, trust, or conversion.

## Later-Phase Candidates

CRM integrations may be considered if individual inquiry volume and follow-up needs justify them.

Corporate services are not currently offered. Corporate landing pages, workshop catalogs, inquiry workflows, and case studies are not part of the current roadmap. Any future corporate offering requires a separate business decision before product work begins.

## Roadmap Rule

Future capabilities may be anticipated through extension points and interfaces, but they should not be implemented before they are needed.
