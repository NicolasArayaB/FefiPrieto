# Roadmap

## Purpose

This document defines the product roadmap at a capability level. It prevents future-phase ideas from leaking into the MVP before they are justified.

## Initial Content

## MVP Purpose

The MVP exists to validate:

* Positioning
* Messaging
* Lead generation
* Brand authority

## MVP Scope

The MVP should support:

* Premium brand storytelling.
* Clear explanation of image psychology.
* Corporate lead generation.
* Individual booking generation.
* Service exploration.
* Trust-building content.
* Performance-focused responsive experience.

## Explicitly Out of MVP Scope

Do not introduce:

* CMS integrations
* Blog infrastructure
* CRM integrations
* User authentication
* User accounts
* Payment processing
* E-commerce
* Membership systems
* Online courses
* Dashboards
* Client portals
* Complex admin panels
* Multi-language support
* Webinar platforms
* AI-powered personalization
* Mobile applications

## Phase 2 Candidates

Phase 2 may include:

* Educational articles
* Downloadable resources
* Newsletter
* Expanded testimonials
* CMS adoption if justified

These should be added only after MVP learning shows they support authority, trust, or conversion.

## Phase 3 Candidates

Phase 3 may include:

* Corporate landing pages
* Workshop catalog
* Corporate inquiry workflows
* CRM integrations
* Corporate case studies

These should be added only after corporate demand and sales workflow requirements are clearer.

## Roadmap Rule

Future capabilities may be anticipated through extension points and interfaces, but they should not be implemented before they are needed.
