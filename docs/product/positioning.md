# Positioning

## Purpose

This document translates the product vision into practical positioning guidance for website content, service presentation, and brand differentiation.

## Initial Content

## Positioning Statement

Fefi Prieto is a premium psychology-informed image consulting brand that helps individuals and organizations align identity, confidence, and external expression to strengthen presence, authority, and personal transformation.

## Primary Positioning

The initial positioning is:

> Psychology + Image Consulting

Executive presence and leadership development should appear as natural extensions of this expertise, not as disconnected or premature claims.

## Differentiation

Fefi Prieto should be differentiated from:

* Generic styling services
* Traditional image consulting marketplaces
* Fashion e-commerce brands
* Generic coaching platforms
* Motivational content brands
* Generic leadership consultancies

The brand should feel educational, psychological, premium, and transformational.

## Message Hierarchy

Lead with:

* Image is psychological.
* Confidence affects behavior and performance.
* Identity and external expression should be aligned.
* Executive presence can be intentionally developed.

Support with:

* Consulting and coaching services.
* Corporate workshops and executive presence programs.
* Individual image consulting and personal branding services.

Avoid leading with:

* Fashion trends.
* Clothing as aesthetic consumption.
* Generic confidence language without psychological framing.
* Broad leadership consulting claims before credibility is established.

## Relationship to Dress the City

Dress the City and Fefi Prieto are complementary but independent. Dress the City addresses what someone should wear for important moments. Fefi Prieto addresses who someone becomes through image, confidence, and identity alignment.
