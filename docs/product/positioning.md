# Positioning

## Purpose

This document translates the product vision into practical positioning guidance for website content, service presentation, and brand differentiation.

## Initial Content

## Positioning Statement

Fernanda Prieto is a premium psychology-informed image consulting brand that helps individuals align identity, confidence, and external expression to support personal transformation. The primary audience is women around ages 30–50 seeking personal image consulting.

## Primary Positioning

The initial positioning is:

> Psychology + Image Consulting

Executive presence and leadership development remain possible future directions, not current services or the focus of audience messaging. Corporate services are not currently offered.

## Differentiation

Fernanda Prieto should be differentiated from:

* Generic styling services
* Traditional image consulting marketplaces
* Fashion e-commerce brands
* Generic coaching platforms
* Motivational content brands
* Generic leadership consultancies

The brand should feel educational, psychological, premium, and transformational.

## Message Hierarchy

Lead with:

* Image is psychological.
* Confidence affects behavior and performance.
* Identity and external expression should be aligned.
* Personal image can express identity and support everyday confidence.

Support with:

* The individual services confirmed in [services.md](services.md).
* Clear explanations of what each service includes and how to inquire.

Avoid leading with:

* Fashion trends.
* Clothing as aesthetic consumption.
* Generic confidence language without psychological framing.
* Broad leadership consulting claims before credibility is established.

## Relationship to Dress the City

Dress the City and Fernanda Prieto are complementary but independent. Dress the City addresses what someone should wear for important moments. Fernanda Prieto addresses who someone becomes through image, confidence, and identity alignment.

For detailed relationship guidance, use [vision.md](vision.md).
