# Homepage Editorial Coherence

## Narrative Sequence

The homepage moves from Fernanda's point of view to the way she works, the services she offers, evidence from clients, and finally her personal authority. The corporate inquiry is the primary conversion path; the Individual route remains present as a secondary, clearly differentiated path inside Services.

## Rhythm

The sections alternate their visual role:

1. **Hero:** identity statement with one orange photographic field and the primary corporate action.
2. **Methodology:** quiet, structured explanation in three stages.
3. **Services:** the longest editorial chapter, using photography, cobalt, and orange to distinguish Corporate from Individual.
4. **Testimonials:** a single-column entry creates a reading pause before the expressive proof block.
5. **Authority:** neutral portraiture and personal context close the narrative.

Services uses standard section spacing because its internal chapters already create an extended visual sequence. This avoids a second oversized pause before Testimonials while preserving a meaningful section transition.

## Guardrails

* Do not repeat the same two-column heading pattern in adjacent sections.
* Reserve large Canela styles for the Hero, major editorial statements, featured service, and authority title.
* Keep expressive color tied to a specific narrative role: orientation in Hero, service distinction in Services, and proof in Testimonials.
* Use editorial rules only for chapter or content transitions; whitespace should otherwise carry the sequence.
* No final CTA section exists yet. Do not add one until its message and conversion destination are defined.
