# Design Principles

## Purpose

This document defines the experience principles that should guide visual design, interaction design, and frontend implementation.

## Initial Content

## Business First

Every feature must support:

* Lead generation.
* Brand authority.
* User trust.
* Conversion.

Features that do not contribute to business goals should be questioned.

## Storytelling Over Information Dumping

The website should guide users through a narrative journey rather than presenting large amounts of information simultaneously.

## Premium Simplicity

Sophistication should emerge through:

* Typography.
* Spacing.
* Composition.
* Motion.

Avoid visual complexity for its own sake.

## Motion With Purpose

Animations should support:

* Understanding.
* Hierarchy.
* Storytelling.
* Emotional impact.

Never animate solely for decoration.

## Editorial Design

The experience should feel closer to a premium editorial experience than a traditional corporate website.

## Performance Is A Feature

Visual ambition must never compromise:

* Responsiveness.
* Accessibility.
* Usability.

## Mobile Is First-Class

Mobile experiences should receive the same attention as desktop experiences.

## Future-Proof Content

Business definitions will evolve. The design should support changing services, proof points, and content modules without requiring major structural rewrites.
