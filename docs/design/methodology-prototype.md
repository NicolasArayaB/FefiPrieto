# Methodology Prototype Notes

## Purpose

FEF-15 validates an editorial Methodology section around the sequence Psychology → Coaching → Image Consulting. Its working copy and structure are intentionally provisional; the goal is to test hierarchy, rhythm, and conceptual clarity.

## Decisions Validated

* A display-led introduction gives the section a distinct rhythm from the Hero while maintaining the same typography and color language.
* The three stages are expressed as an ordered, ruled sequence rather than cards or panels. This makes the progression explicit without adding surface elevation.
* Each stage uses the editorial Grid to pair the conceptual title with reading-width explanatory copy. At the 48rem breakpoint, the same source order becomes a title-then-description sequence.
* The section transition uses a single subtle rule and editorial spacing. No new background, surface, radius, or shadow treatment is needed.

## Deferred Decisions

* The ruled sequence is evidence for DS005 review, not a generalized surface or separator abstraction.
* A progressive reveal while scrolling could reinforce the stage relationship, but it remains a DS006 consideration; the section communicates fully without motion.
* Final methodology language, supporting proof, and imagery remain outside this prototype.
